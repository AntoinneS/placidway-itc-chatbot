    How grounding works (read this before you test it)

    1) Ingest: ingest.py re-fetches only the 7 URLs above (~1 request/second, clear User-Agent) and saves a clean text snapshot. 2) Knowledge base: knowledge-base.json holds short chunks, each checked against the page text, with its source URL. 3) Retrieve: the bot tokenises your question, scores chunks by keyword / phrase overlap (with a small synonym map, e.g. cost = price), and only answers from the best chunk(s) above a threshold. 4) Guardrails first: booking, weather, personal medical advice, and prices not in the pages are handled by fixed rules before retrieval — they can never be “answered” by inventing something. 5) Refresh: re-run ingest.py, diff the snapshots, update knowledge-base.json, re-run the 8 tests, update the “Last updated” stamp.

    What this bot cannot do — honestly

    It cannot book appointments, give a diagnosis or say whether a treatment is safe for you, quote a price that is not on these 7 pages, compare a price in a country that is not listed for that treatment, or describe a video beyond its title (no transcripts were in the extracted page content). For any of those, it points you to a free PlacidWay quote instead.
